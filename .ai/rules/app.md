# App

## Vendor points, ranking weights and violation ladder are fixed by the kertas kerja
Vendor points: complete profile +50, complete catalogue +30, booking via platform +100, deposit paid +100, booking completed +150, full payment +150, positive review +20, fast response +20. No points for enquiries alone.
Ranking tiers: New -> Verified -> Trusted -> Top -> Recommended.
Recommended Vendor score: rating 30%, completed bookings 20%, completion rate 15%, response rate 15%, platform transactions 10%, profile & catalogue quality 10%. Rating alone must never drive ranking.
Payment-bypass violations: 1st warning, 2nd point deduction + ranking drop, 3rd temporary suspension, repeated removal. Admin reviews customer reports before action.

## Payments go to the vendor; Neekah only records or links them
No booking money ever moves through Neekah. Online bookings (Pro, see the VendorAvailability rule in support.md) take a deposit into the vendor's own account: through the vendor's own Herepay (StartDepositPayment → link; ConfirmOnlineDeposit is the only thing that confirms from a callback) or by transfer to the vendor's bank details (the manual flow below). Everything else is manual. The couple deals with the vendor directly, then records what they paid (amount, paid_on, optional receipt image) on their booking; the payment lands as PaymentStatus::AwaitingVerification and changes nothing until the vendor confirms it, because only the vendor can see their own account. VerifyManualPayment is what awards points and flips a PendingPayment booking to Confirmed.

bookings.deposit_amount is back (25 Sep 2026) but only as the VENDOR's own rule (VendorBookingSetting deposit_type percent|fixed), stamped by CreateBooking on online bookings; bookings a vendor records carry none. Neekah never invents a split. An online booking holds its date until hold_expires_at (OnlineBookingSettings::holdHours, default 24h); neekah:expire-booking-holds cancels unpaid ones (CancellationReason::Expired, canceller null) unless a receipt awaits the vendor. Online bookings earn PlatformBooking points at confirmation, not creation. CreateBooking locks the vendor row and rechecks availability, so capacity cannot be exceeded. Booking::paidAmount() counts verified payments only; outstandingAmount() is what is left.

A couple may cancel their own booking (BookingPolicy::cancel) only while no payment is verified. A vendor may cancel any active booking of theirs (BookingPolicy::vendorCancel); a paid deposit is then theirs to refund directly, and they record it with PaymentStatus::Refunded — Neekah has no refund API. CancelBooking revokes the points that booking earned. Admin → Tetapan → Bayaran tempahan (PaymentSettings) shows a switch only for methods where App\Enums\PaymentMethod::isIntegrated() is true (today only manual transfer); switching it off hides the record form. Neekah holds no bank account of its own, so there are no bank fields. A gateway is only offered once PaymentMethod::isIntegrated() returns true for it — switching one on before its checkout is built does nothing. Adding a gateway = a new enum case (its settingKey is picked up by the settings form automatically) plus its integration.

## A couple account switches to vendor only while it has no couple activity
Users have one role and emails are unique, so a vendor who signed up as a couple could not use /vendor/register. /vendor/tukar-akaun (Vendor\AccountConversionController → RegisterVendor::convert) turns the same account into a pending vendor, gated by User::canBecomeVendor(): customer role and no wedding, booking, enquiry or review. Anything with activity goes through admin, so no couple data ends up owned by a vendor. RegisterVendorRequest skips customers in its email unique rule and adds an `existing_customer` error instead, which VendorRegisterForm.vue shows with a "log masuk & tukar akaun" link. Covered by VendorAccountConversionTest.

## Admin account tools refuse anything that loses records
Admin → Pengguna → {user} (admin.users.show) offers switch couple→vendor, vendor→couple, deactivate/reactivate and delete, each gated by UserPolicy and never on admin accounts. Delete is refused when the account has bookings (either side) or created a wedding shared with another member, because cascades would erase someone else's data — deactivate instead. users.deactivated_at is enforced by EnsureAccountIsActive in the web group (signs the session out on its next request); deactivating a vendor also suspends the profile, and reactivating does not re-approve it. File cleanup on delete goes through RemoveVendorProfile/DeleteUserAccount, since DB cascades leave images on disk. Covered by UserAccountToolsTest.

## One account per person: account page vs vendor business profile
Personal details (name, phone, email, password) are edited at /akaun (Auth\AccountController), one page rendered inside whichever dashboard layout the role uses; the vendor's business profile stays in the vendor area. Changing email requires current_password because email is the password-reset key; a Google account (no password) sets a first one without it, gated by User::hasPassword(). A password change calls Auth::logoutOtherDevices, so it actually ends someone else's access. Vendors also have a `logo` (vendors.logo, Vendor::logoUrl(), x-vendor-avatar) that falls back to the initial in a cover_tone circle — the public page had no way to change that circle before. Covered by AccountSettingsTest and VendorLogoTest.

## Neekah Pro is paid placement beside the ranking, never inside it
Owner, 23 Sep 2026: vendors may buy Neekah Pro (monthly/yearly, prices in ProSettings, Admin → Tetapan → Pro). It is a vendor subscription, not a commission and not couple-to-vendor money, so "Yuran platform: Percuma" stays true. Pro buys three things only: the labelled "Ditaja" row above page 1 of the default sort (VendorController::sponsored, same filters as the listing), analytics (vendor_daily_stats, counted for every vendor, full chart for Pro), and the Pro badge. It must never touch tier, score or the ordinary listing order. vendors.pro_until is the single source; ActivateVendorPro is the only thing that marks a VendorSubscription paid (idempotent, adds onto remaining time), for the Herepay webhook and admin manual records alike. Checkout stays closed until ProSettings enabled AND HerepayClient::isConfigured(). Covered by VendorProTest, SponsoredVendorsTest, VendorReachTest.
